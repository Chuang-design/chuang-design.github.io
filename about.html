About

I am a PhD candidate at Zhejiang University’s College of Artificial Intelligence, advised by Prof. Guanyun Wang at Guanyun Lab.

My research focuses on personal fabrication as a way to empower people through customized and adaptive production. I work across fabrication methods, smart material systems, tangible interaction, and sustainable design.

I envision a future in which fabrication technologies help people create objects that respond to individual needs while using materials and resources more responsibly.
